# iblock-for-all-ads
iBlock is a focused ad-blocking extension built to reduce clutter, stop intrusive advertising, and improve usability across video, streaming, anime, forum, shopping, and general browsing websites.

Unlike simple cosmetic blockers that only hide a few visible page elements, iBlock is designed to target the ad behaviors that commonly make websites frustrating to use: banner ads, linked image ads, animated ad panels, popup triggers, tab-under behavior, and many promotional blocks placed around media players and page content.

The extension includes two blocking modes so users can choose how aggressive the filtering should be depending on the website they are visiting.

Regular Mode
Regular Mode is designed for everyday browsing. It focuses on blocking common ad content while trying to preserve normal website layout and page functionality. This mode is intended to remove visible banner ads, promotional image boxes, many GIF-based ad placements, popup-style click traps, and common ad containers without being overly aggressive on general websites.

Super Mode
Super Mode is stronger and intended for websites that are heavily saturated with intrusive ads or self-hosted promotional blocks. It applies broader filtering and stronger page-level cleanup for sites where normal ad blocking is often not enough. This mode is useful on obscure or ad-heavy websites, but because it is more aggressive, some websites may display layout issues until the site is whitelisted.

What iBlock is built to help with

Banner ads displayed around page content
Linked image advertisements
Animated ad images and visual promo blocks
Sidebar and under-player sponsored blocks
Popup and tab-under style click traps
Many self-hosted ad elements on obscure websites
General page clutter caused by ad-heavy layouts

Whitelist support
iBlock includes a built-in whitelist system so you can disable blocking on specific websites. This is useful for pages that require ads or promotional scripts to remain enabled for certain features to function normally, or for websites you want to support directly. The whitelist supports up to 50 websites.

Site logging
The extension can store a lightweight local log of websites where likely video-related ad activity was detected. Each website is only stored once rather than being repeatedly added across multiple visits. This log is stored locally in extension storage for convenience and review.

Designed for usability
A major goal of iBlock is to reduce the common frustration of websites that fill the page with distracting ads outside the main content area. It is especially aimed at users who browse video-heavy sites, anime streaming sites, general entertainment pages, and other websites where ads often appear in sidebars, content rails, or under media players.

Important note
iBlock is designed as a best-effort ad blocker and page cleaner. Some modern websites use complex or server-delivered advertising systems, and some websites actively react to blockers. Because of how websites differ, results may vary depending on the platform, layout, and ad delivery method.

Current features

Two blocking modes: Regular and Super
Whitelist support for up to 50 websites
Local site log for detected video-ad related activity
Blocking for many banner, image, and promo-style ads
Popup and intrusive click-trap reduction
Stronger filtering for ad-heavy and obscure websites
